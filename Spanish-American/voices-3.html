She Arrives at Montauk

Miss Robinson on the ground where Company L boys are probably now encamped.

Most God Forsaken Place She Ever Saw.

Company was just disembarking when she wrote.

Mrs. Wilson writes describing the boys in hospital of Boston.

All the Boys Were Not There Yet.

Captain J.E. Ball received yesterday a letter from Miss Robinson, the nurse who was sent down to Montauk Point by the relief societies of Marquette to do what she could do personally for Co. L and to spend the money given her to the best advantage of the boys.

The letter, which was written Aug. 25, reads as follows:

“To Captain Ball — I arrived here today after starting Mrs. Wilson to Boston. Such a God forsaken place I never saw.”

“A railway comes into Montauk, but there are no houses within fifteen miles. East Hampton is the nearest place. Some people come and go from New York every day. They come in at noon and return 7:50 PM. It is three and one half hours from New York.”
