Korea: Axing Incident

On August 18, 1976 an Army tree-trimming crew entered the DMZ to trim a poplar tree that obstructed the view from a U.N. checkpoint. North Korean soldiers arrived and attacked the small crew with axes and clubs. Two American soldiers were killed and others wounded. The attack became known as the Ax Murder Incident.

At 0700 hours on August 21, South Korean Special Forces accompanied the American tree-trimming party back to the poplar tree. This time 64 South Korean Taekwondo masters carrying M16 rifles, 12 Army engineers equipped with chain saws, and 20 US/ROK guards armed with semi-automatic pistols and axe handles were supported by helicopter gunships, Air Force F-4 Phantoms and F-111 fighter/bombers, and B-52 bombers. The engineers cut the tree down. The mission was called OPERATION PAUL BUNYAN.
