Korea:
Axing Incident,
August 18, 1976

The Cold War is sometimes called the war in which no bullets were fired. Even without considering Vietnam and Korea, there were many incidents, deaths, injuries, and prisoners taken. In one example, North Korean guards beat two U.S. Army officers to death and wounded several more in the demilitarized zone.

The two American officers, a South Korean officer, and several American enlisted men were escorting Korean Service Corps workers trimming trees in Panmunjom. After North Korean objections, additional guards arrived and the attack followed. Marquette resident John Moschetti was stationed near the DMZ at the time.
