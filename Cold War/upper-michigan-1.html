K.I. Sawyer Air Force Base, 1956–1995

Strategic Air Command used K.I. Sawyer, flying massive B-52 Stratofortress bombers and KC-135 air refueling tankers in 1960. Later, the 87th FIS flew F-106 Delta Dart interceptors. The base could track and intercept enemy bombers with SAGE. Sawyer was home to the 410th Bomb Wing, known as one of the best bomb units for its accuracy.
