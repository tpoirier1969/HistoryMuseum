ELF

ELF, or Extremely Low Frequency Radio, was designed to help the U.S. Navy communicate with its submarine fleet. First named Project Sanguine and then Project Seafarer, ELF was a smaller version of the original proposals. The project was controversial because of cost, usefulness, health and environmental concerns, and questions about first-strike nuclear strategy. Nevertheless, ELF went ahead and operated from 1989 to 2004.
