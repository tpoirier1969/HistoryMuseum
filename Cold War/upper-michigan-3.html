Oscoda/Kincheloe Air Force Station, 1951–1977

The base was built during World War II, put into civilian use after the war, and resumed being an Air Force Base in 1951. Then called Oscoda Air Force Base, it was renamed Kincheloe in 1959. A Strategic Air Command base, the Air Force extended the runway to 12,000 feet for 15 B-52 bombers and ten KC-135 tankers. The 4239th Squadron was stationed there.
