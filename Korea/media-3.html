Timeline for the Korean War

After World War II, Korea was divided along the 38th Parallel with Soviet forces accepting the surrender of the Japanese in the North and American forces disarming the Japanese in the South. The Soviets set up a communist government and began to build and train the North Korean People’s Army.

June 26, 1950

North Korea and the Soviets invade South Korea. U.S. ground forces are poorly trained or equipped and so are unable to stop the T34 tanks. The UN Security Council sends troops to support the U.S.
