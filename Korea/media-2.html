Korean War

The Allied victory over fascism ended WWII but it did not ensure global peace. Even before the last shots of WWII had been fired, a new and even greater evil threaten the existence of the free world. Communism under the leadership of the dictators Stalin and Mao would plunge the world in to a forty five year conflict know as the Cold War. Starting with an attempt to overthrow the Greek government, the occupation of eastern Europe, an invasion of Iran and the threaten invasion of Turkey, Stalin ignited the Cold War in Europe while in China the resumption of the Chinese Civil War would spread communism through Asia. The First Indo China War, the Malayan Emergency, the invasion of Tibet and the Korean War were the early campaigns in the Cold War.
