Copper Mining

The Quincy Mine produced 1,251 tons of copper in 1864. The Quincy was the top producer of the 34 mines in the Lake Superior region at this time.

Copper was used in making percussion caps, buckles, and new telegraph lines. As the war continued, demand grew, increasing both the output and price of copper. Sixty-eight million pounds of copper came out of Michigan’s Lake Superior mines during the Civil War. A war-time copper tariff also reduced copper imports.
