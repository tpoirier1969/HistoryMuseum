105 mm Howitzer

It was modified after its use in World War II to increase its mobility in the jungle. It could be towed behind a truck or by helicopter. 8 men operated it. It accepted a variety of ammunition, from high explosive shrapnel shells to “beehive” cartridges with thousands of small sharpened darts. With a range of 12,500 yards and 3-8 rounds per minute, it was an extremely deadly weapon.
