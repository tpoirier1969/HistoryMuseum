Boeing B-52 Stratofortress

In Vietnam it was outfitted in Thailand and Guam with conventional bombs. It did not have the effect on the enemy the way it might have in another war. However, it contained or broke up North Vietnam offensives, disrupted supply lines, and helped bring about negotiations. With a crew of six, it held 60,000 pounds of bombs and was armed with four M2 .50 caliber machine guns.
