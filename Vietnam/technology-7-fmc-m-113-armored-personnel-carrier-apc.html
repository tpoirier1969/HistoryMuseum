FMC M-113 Armored Personnel Carrier (APC)

Also built by United Defense LP Ground Systems FMC is the Food Machinery Corp.

The M113 was an important transport used in many types of terrain, including water, making it valuable in the jungle. The two-man crew could move 11 soldiers. It was equipped with 12-38 mm of armor, a .50 cal Browning machine gun, and two optional M60 machine guns.
