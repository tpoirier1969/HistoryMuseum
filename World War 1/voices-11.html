Movement Home
12 November 1918 - 23 May 1919

The Great War came to an end and the 32nd Division moved to the American Occupation Sector near Coblenz in December 1918, remaining there until April 1919. After completing its occupation duties in Germany, the division moved to Brest, France. Beginning on April 20, 1919, units of the division began their trip home.

The last of the division's troops arrived in New York on May 22. The 64th Infantry Brigade demobilized at Camp Grant, Illinois on May 17 and the 63rd Infantry Brigade and division troops demobilized at Camp Custer, Michigan on May 23. During the war, the division fought in three major offensives, suffered over 14,000 casualties, and was the first American unit to set foot on German soil. The men of the 32nd Division returned home to well-earned displays of appreciation for their service and sacrifice.
