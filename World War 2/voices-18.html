June 21, 1943... Good pilots can fly anything.

"Boots" Kukuk

June 21, 43
Awoke this morning at 6:30 A.M. with general alarm. I found out that thirty Jap planes were seen over Cactus coming in our direction. All 32 fighters were launched and flew all day, stopping just long enough to refuel and change pilots.
I flew from 1430 to 1745 and landed aboard after sunset. We saw a couple of Jap bombers, but they had more altitude and we couldn’t catch them even after dropping our wing tanks.
We have old planes off the Enterprise and Saratoga, and we shouldn’t even be in the combat zone with them. The engines have all but worn out completely. They say a good pilot can fly the crates the planes come in. But for my personal money I’ll fly the crates they come in – (that is if I had the rank to choose between them). Every day we have an average of seven forced landings abord carrier because of mechanical failure. Right now this is a military secret, but the day will come when the men back home will put out a plane that will be new and strong to best the Jap. So until that day, we will trudge along with what little material we have and help of God.
