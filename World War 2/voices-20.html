July 9, 1943...22 of 32 planes return from mission

"Boots" Kukuk

July 9, 1943
We went up to Kahili Bomber strip on escort of B-24's, TBF's, SBD's and a mass of 74U's, a few F-30?s and of course our lowly Grummans.
On the first raid I was still on my way back from water experience by destroyer, When I got back I waited anxiously for their return. Out of 32 planes only 22 planes and pilots came back. Some where seen shot down, some were seen in water in rubber boats. Our score was fifteen zeros to ten Grummens, which we knew wasn’t too good. But the bombers sunk a cruiser, two destroyers and bombed field.
The next raid we went on was a complete success. We lost one pilot and plane through accident. Two of our planes came together in air over Kahili when a wingman turned nose upward to shoot at a zero. The leader finally returned to Sagi and bailed out, the wingman just went down ay Kahili; and is probably now a Japanese prisoner.
When we were on the raid we could see about 50- or 60 zeros above us and one would dive down and then pull sharply out of our gun fire. They would not attack us though because they did not have us at three to one odds like they usually did in a fight. Our bombers wrecked the place; so they had to spend a few days digging out of the ruins.
