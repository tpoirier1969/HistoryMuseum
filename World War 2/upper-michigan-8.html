Cliffs Dow Chemical Company

The company produced charcoal and wood-based chemicals which were shipped to defense plants from Chicago to the east coast. It used more wood than any other plant in the U.S. in its production of charcoal and chemicals. Charcoal was used in making steel rollers for mills, in the manufacture of alloy steel for armor plate and in the processing of non-ferrous alloys. Chemicals produced at Cliffs Dow included methyl acetone, used in making explosives and inhibitor oil, which was used in converting low grade gas into an excellent motor fuel.
