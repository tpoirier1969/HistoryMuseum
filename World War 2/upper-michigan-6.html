Production

The War Production Board, which oversaw war industries, set up a contract distribution office in Iron Mountain. It aided large and small industries in converting to war production.

Iron

The mines on the Marquette Range increased their production fourfold in the first year of the war. 11 million tons of iron ore were produced in Michigan during World War II. Iron was essential for industrial production of firearms, ships, automobiles and tanks. Most of the nation’s iron ore (85%) came from the Lake Superior region (including Minnesota), although about 14% came from Michigan.
