News Headlines

Every city, big or small, had its own newspaper, which was fairly inexpensive. The Detroit News cost 3 cents for the daily edition and 10 cents for the Sunday edition. Headlines, in big bold lettering, screamed out the latest war news. For example:

GERMANY INVADES POLAND, BRITAIN DECLARES WAR!, JAPS – U.S. AT WAR INVASION, and V E – DAY!, are just a few of the thousands of wartime headlines that captured the attention of readers.
