News Headlines

Every city, big or small, had its own newspaper which were fairly inexpensive. The Detroit News cost 3 cents for the daily edition and 10 cents for the Sunday edition. Headlines, in big bold lettering screamed out the latest war news. For example: GERMANY INVADES POLAND, BRITAIN DECLARES WAR!, JAPS – U.S. AT WAR INVASION, and V E – DAY! are just a few of the thousands of war time headlines that captured the attention of readers.

  1. Sept. 1, 1939: Germany invades Poland.
  2. June 14, 1940: Paris is captured and France surrenders after only days of Blitzkrieg.
  3. Dec. 7, 1941: Japan bombs Pearl Harbor, bringing the United States into war.
  4. Dec. 8, 1941: Japan declares war.
  5. Victory!
