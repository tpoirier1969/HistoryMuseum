Victory Gardens

Nationwide, about 20 million gardeners produced 1/11th of the food supply and 42% of the fresh vegetables eaten in the country. Communities across the country provided areas for individuals to stake out their own gardening plots. In Marquette, the plots were located on West College Ave. The city paid for plowing, but individual gardeners were responsible for fertilizers, seeds and maintenance. In Michigan, 720,000 gardeners produced 20 million bushels of vegetables by 1944.
