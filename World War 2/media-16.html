Entertaining the Troops and the USO

The United Service Organization, USO, was created by President Roosevelt on 4 February 1942. He determined a private organization should provide the entertainment of the rapidly growing military. Six civilian agencies, the Salvation Army, YMCA, YWCA, National Catholic Community Services, National Travelers Aid Association and the National Jewish Welfare Board coordinated their civilian war efforts to form the USO. During the war the USO became the G.I.s “home away from home” and began a tradition of providing entertainment for the troops that continues today.

Comedian Bob Hope performed his first USO tour in 1942 and continued to entertain the troops into the 1990s. He and thousands of entertainers provided wholesome entertainment to millions of G.I.s around the world.
