Hollywood and World War II

Army Chief of Staff General George C. Marshall commissioned Hollywood director Frank Capra to explain the war to millions of soldiers in a series of seven one-hour orientation films entitled Why We Fight. Capra’s propaganda films divided the world neatly into forces of light and darkness to make clear the dangers of totalitarianism. The Why We Fight series was the most elaborate statement of war aims produced by any part of the federal government during World War II.
