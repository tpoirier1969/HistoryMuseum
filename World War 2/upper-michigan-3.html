Inmates

Inmates from the Marquette branch prison watched for planes from the prison’s aircraft spotting tower at the Honor Camp in Harvey.

Two observers were on each three hour shift; volunteers took one shift every two weeks. Volunteers served in all Upper Peninsula counties. The state wide system was aimed at protecting industrial plants and transportation, such as the Soo Locks. Here the mines, forests, docks, mills and railroads were all important resources for the war.
