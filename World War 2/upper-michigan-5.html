Guarding the Locks

The locks at Sault Ste. Marie were essential in keeping the flow of ore carriers to the steels mills. 15,000 soldiers were stationed at Fort Brady and were charged with guarding the locks. Large barrage balloons and anti-aircraft guns were installed to prevent enemy attack by air.
