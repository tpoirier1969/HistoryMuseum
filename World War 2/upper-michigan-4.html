Ore Docks and Trestle

Two days after Pearl Harbor was attacked, the governor of Michigan ordered the State Police and Michigan National Guard to watch the ore docks until private guards could be hired to protect essential facilities. State troopers guarded the ore docks at Marquette and Escanaba.

The Lake Superior and Ishpeming trestle which spans the Dead River outside of Marquette presented another strategic location. Patrols were assigned to guard that as well.
