July 21, 1943
Japs swimming

July 21, 1943

At three in the afternoon, Lt. (J.G.) Tavernetti with Kukuk as wingman and Allan other wingman strafed Bairiki Harbor. When we dove over harbor we saw about 18 Japs in swimming. We opened up our guns and watched them scamper. I guess we killed a few in the water, and we also strafed a few landing barrages. The woods was full of Japs; so we knew our stray bullets massacred many a yellow bodies from Tokyo.
