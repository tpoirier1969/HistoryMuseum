V-J Day

The announcement of Japanese surrender and the end of World War II was met with a huge celebration here and around the country. A large spontaneous celebration on August 14, 1945 erupted with bells and a variety of noisemakers. Thousands of people crowded Marquette’s downtown with marching, singing and fireworks. Most businesses closed for the day. Over fifty vehicles joined in the parade as well as children on decorated bicycles. An ice cream truck carried the sign, “You’ll have more of me now.” The Mining Journal called the parade the largest Marquette had seen. Ishpeming and Negaunee also had joyful celebrations with parades and noisemakers.
