Boeing B-17 Flying Fortress

Brought into service in June of 1939, the B-17 was the most advanced bomber in the world and would prove to be the work horse of the USAAF during the war. It carried a crew of 10: pilot, co-pilot, bombardier, navigator, radio operator, engineer and four gunners. It was powered by four 1,200 hp Wright Cyclone engines and had a max speed of 287 mph with a ceiling of 35,000 feet and range of 1,100 miles when fully loaded with 12,800 pounds of bombs. It had ten .50 caliber machine guns for defense.
