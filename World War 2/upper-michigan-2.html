Guarding Important Resources
Civil Aircraft Warning Service (cont.)

In Marquette the post was a shelter on top of Graveraet High School. It was heated, lighted and equipped with the required telephone. The first watch fell on October 5, 1942.

Soon winter chills prompted residents to donate fur coats for the observers at the school.

In Ishpeming a post was set up on the county road commission office in November of 1942. A post in Republic on Kloman hill opened late that year. It was an army station, but also used volunteer observers.
