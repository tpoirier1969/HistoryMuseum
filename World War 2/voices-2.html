Nancy Harkness Love

Born in Houghton and a skilled pilot, Love began the civilian group Women’s Auxiliary Ferrying Squad (WASPS). These pilots flew all over the continental U.S. They were continually upgraded so that they could fly every plane from the P-51 Mustang fighters to the Super Fortress. Although never accepted into the military, Love had uniforms made for her pilots.
