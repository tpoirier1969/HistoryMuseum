Recruiting Posters

The Armed Forces used recruiting posters to attract young men and women to their respective services. The posters often extolled the proud history of the service or the role Americans had played in the defense of freedom. Other themes appealed to citizens to join for the sake of their country and build a better future for themselves. Potential soldiers were not the only target of government posters. All citizens and especially those working in war-related industries were urged to stay on the job and keep producing the material the Armed Forces would need to win the war.
