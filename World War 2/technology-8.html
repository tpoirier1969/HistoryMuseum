Douglas C-47 Skytrain and Waco Glider

During World War II, the armed forces of many Allied countries used the C -47 for the transport of paratroops and towing gliders. The C-47 could carry 18-22 fully-equipped paratroopers or tow a WACO C G4A Glider.

Paratroopers and Glider Troopers of WWII were authorized the Parachutist or Glider Badges in recognition of the skills and dangers their service encompassed.
