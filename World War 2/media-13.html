Cartoon Strips - Sad Sack meets Willie and Joe

Two U.S. Army cartoonists, Bill Mauldin and George Baker, cheered millions of GIs with their humorous and often poignant portrayals of Army life. Baker’s Sad Sack strip appeared in Yank Magazine and reached an estimated 10 million readers. Mauldin portrayed the hardships of two infantrymen, Willie and Joe, as they soldiered through the war in Europe.
