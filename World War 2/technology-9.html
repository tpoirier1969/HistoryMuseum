Kelly Johnson

Clarence Leonard "Kelly" Johnson of Ishpeming, led the team that designed the P-38 Lightning.

NAPALM

LOCKHEED P-38’s drop napalm fire bombs on enemy positions in the mountains east of Manila. As each bomb hit the target or ground it would explode and burn everything over an oval-shaped area of approximately 70 by 150 feet. The bombs were effective in eliminating the enemy troops in their well-dug-in positions.
