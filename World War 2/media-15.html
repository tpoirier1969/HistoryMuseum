Actors Who Became Heroes

Not until the late 1930s did Hollywood begin to focus on the growing threats of the Nazis and Japanese. By 1940 Hollywood began to produce pro-interventionist films such as Sergeant York. With the bombing of Pearl Harbor, Hollywood began to produce an enormous number of war-related movies. While not always historically accurate, these movies helped inspire the population to support the war. Some, such as Casablanca, Wake Island, and The Story of G.I. Joe, have withstood the passage of time and are now considered classics.

Not only did the movie industry unconditionally support the war effort, some of its most famous stars took part in the war. Many of Hollywood’s leading actors interrupted their careers to serve in the military. Some stayed in Hollywood, such as Captain Ronald Reagan who made training films for the Army.
