Walt Maki

Having learned to fly from Sig Wilson at Green Garden Airport, Walt enlisted in the Army Air Corps. He patrolled the West Coast in a B-26 when Japanese subs were within 20 miles of the coast. He was a fighter pilot in the Pacific in a P-40. He was shot down over Guam. The Japanese assumed he was dead, but with his burns and wounds he managed to make his way back behind American lines.

Born in southern Manitoba, Walt was brought to Upper Michigan when he was orphaned at the age of three.
